Choose a design for the Home page

Eight designs of the same Home page. The words are identical in all eight — only the look changes, so the comparison is fair. Open each one, then pick the one that feels right for the clinic.

How to compare them

  1. Open each design, scroll to the bottom, then come back using the “All styles” button at the bottom-left.
  2. Look at each one on a phone as well, not only on a laptop.
  3. Ignore the grey boxes and the bracketed words — those are details we are still waiting for.
  4. Judge the feeling: does it look like a place you would trust with something personal?
