Peace sign — ten creative versions

All based on the peace sign the doctor sent, none of them the plain textbook version. Each one is drawn by hand as code: no licence to worry about, sharp at any size, tintable to any of our colours, and it costs nothing to load.

1  ·  The ten

Shown solid so you can see them properly. This is not how they would appear on the site — as a watermark they would be far fainter.

OPTION 1

Growing

The lines sprout leaves. Picks up the waves and warm colours already on the site.

OPTION 2

The walking figure

The person from the clinic’s own logo becomes the inside of the sign.

OPTION 3

Mind

The ring is drawn as the folds of a brain — the other half of the logo.

OPTION 4

One line

Drawn in a single unbroken stroke, never lifted. Quiet and modern.

OPTION 5

Brush drawn

Hand-painted, open at the top, thick to thin. Human, not machine-made.

OPTION 6

Made of the word

Built from the word peace, like the doctor’s picture — but calm.

OPTION 7

Soft dots

Made of dots rather than lines. Gentle, and lovely when very faint.

OPTION 8

Ripples

The ring becomes circles spreading outwards, like calm settling.

OPTION 9

Petals

Six petals overlap to form the ring. A flower and a peace sign at once.

OPTION 10

Left open

The ring does not quite close — the way a breath ends. Unfinished on purpose.
